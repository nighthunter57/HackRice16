# Two-minute demo

Use the pinned September 12, 2026 demo, $449 Sony headphones, a $400 reserve and 60 days. Refresh to restore the first render; the repair toggle never mutates the seed.

| Time | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 0–10s | “Your bank tells you what you have. It doesn't tell you how much is already spoken for.” |
| 10–25s | Show $1,850. Scan a price tag with configured Gemini, confirm $449, or use manual entry. |
| 25–40s | Show rent, phone, the spending forecast from Tiger and the September 17 paycheck. |
| 40–65s | Compare $58.95 minimum buying today with $507.95 waiting. The reserve is $400; goal effects are calculated. |
| 65–80s | Reveal September 17, five days away. Safe maximum today is $107.95. |
| 80–100s | Add the $430 repair scheduled September 20. Safe Date moves to September 24; the cause panel shows +7 days. |
| 100–115s | Show remembered preferences and record “I’ll wait.” Service chips identify actual live/fallback modes. |
| 115–120s | “Your balance tells you what you have. Spendly tells you when you can safely spend it.” |

Rehearse tests/build and the optional browser smoke test. Configure real sponsor accounts and run the Tiger migration before claiming live integration. In demo mode, configured Tiger can store seeded history and feed its aggregate back to forecasting. Live Nessie data produces its own result; pinned demo dates do not apply to arbitrary accounts. Keep manual entry available. Sign in to the Expo app and link its user to the demo Nessie customer before presenting; keep credentials out of the presentation.
